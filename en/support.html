Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

On first launch, enter both of your names and choose your currency. Tap + on Home, add an account and record your first transaction.

How do I share with my partner?

You both need iCloud turned on, each with your own Apple ID. On Home, tap the people icon → Sharing → “Invite your partner” and send the invitation. Your partner opens it on their iPhone, accepts and chooses who they are in the Ambos.

A change did not show up on the other iPhone.

Changes travel through iCloud when you are online and can take a moment. Open Sharing and tap “Refresh”. Offline, the app keeps working and sends everything once you are back online.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Ambos Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks both iPhones in the shared space, even with different Apple IDs. On a new iPhone, open the Premium screen and tap “Restore purchase”.

What if we do not buy Premium?

You keep using the free plan. Everything you recorded stays, and you can still view, edit, sync and export it.

Which currencies can we use?

One per Ambos: US dollar, euro, British pound, Brazilian real or Mexican peso. Amounts are never converted.

Does it connect to our bank, or work on Android?

No to both. You add transactions yourselves, and Ambos Money is for iPhone (iOS 17 or later).

How do I export my data?

Settings → “Export spreadsheet (CSV)”. The file only goes where you choose.

How do I stop sharing?

Whoever created the share opens Sharing → “Manage sharing” and removes the other person or stops sharing. Data already on each iPhone is not deleted.

How do I delete my data?

Transactions, accounts, goals and cards can be deleted one by one in the app. Deleting the app deletes the data stored on that iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
