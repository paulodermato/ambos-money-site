Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026
Controller (responsible for your data): Paulo de Telles e Batista · Contact: paulotell27@yahoo.com.br

Ambos Money is an app for couples to organize their money together. This policy explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it and what it does not do.

1. What the app stores

Ambos Money stores the information you type in:

  • the names of the two people in the couple and the name of your shared space;
  • accounts (name, type, ownership, starting balance);
  • transactions (amount, description, category, who it belongs to, account, date);
  • goals (name, target amount, amount saved, optional target date);
  • credit cards, if you use them: card name, issuer, brand, last four digits (optional), holder, limit, closing and due days, purchases (amount, installments, date, description, merchant, category, who it belongs to) and statement adjustments. The app does not ask for the full card number, expiration date or security code;
  • the currency chosen for your space.

The app also keeps preferences on the iPhone itself, such as the language you chose and when it last asked for a rating or showed the Premium screen. These preferences are not sent to the developer.

When you share, the app also stores which person in the couple corresponds to each iCloud account, using the identifier iCloud gives the app for each user. This identifier contains no name, email or phone number.

2. Where the data is kept

2.1 On the iPhone

Data is saved in the app’s storage on each iPhone. iPhone backups made by the system (to iCloud or to a computer, depending on each person’s settings) may include this data.

2.2 In iCloud, when you share

Sharing uses Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). Nothing is sent to iCloud until someone taps “Invite your partner”.

  • The person who creates the share keeps the couple’s data (the items in section 1, except the preferences kept on the iPhone) in an Ambos Money area in that person’s iCloud.
  • The invitation is private: only the invited person who accepts it can read and change this data. There is no public access.
  • Each person’s changes are sent to that area and received by the other iPhone.
  • Storage and transmission follow Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy.
  • The developer does not run servers with your financial data and has no access to the data stored in iCloud: according to Apple’s documentation, records in an iCloud private database are not visible to the developer. The only exception is the Ambos Premium license (section 2.5), which contains no financial data.

2.3 Stopping sharing and removing participants

The person who created the share does this in Sharing → “Manage sharing” (a standard iOS screen), where they can remove the other person or stop sharing. The removed person stops sending and receiving changes; data already on their iPhone stays there and is not deleted by the app. The data remains in the iCloud of the person who created the share.

2.4 Spreadsheet export (CSV), started by you

In Settings → “Export spreadsheet (CSV)”, the app creates the files for the active space on your iPhone and opens the iOS share sheet. The file only goes where you choose (Files, AirDrop, Mail, another app); the app does not send it anywhere else. Once shared, the file is the responsibility of whoever receives it. It contains financial data: share it only with people who should see it.

2.5 Ambos Premium and the license service

Ambos Premium is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer does not receive card details, name, email or Apple ID.

To unlock Premium for both people in the couple, even with different Apple IDs, the app uses a license service operated by the developer and hosted on Cloudflare:

  • What is sent: the purchase confirmation signed by Apple and a random identifier of the shared space (generated by the app, unrelated to names or accounts). To renew, at most about once a day, the app sends only the current license.
  • What the service stores: a cryptographic hash of the purchase number, the product, the environment (production or test), the status (active or revoked), dates, the number of transfers, the license’s technical identifier and the random space identifier. It also stores refund notices sent by Apple (notice identifier, type and date) for 90 days.
  • What the service never receives: balances, transactions, accounts, cards, goals, amounts of your money, names, email, Apple ID or iCloud credentials.
  • Where the license is kept: the signed license is kept on the iPhone (Keychain) and in the shared space’s iCloud, with your data, so that the other person also has Premium.
  • Refunds: if Apple refunds the purchase, the service marks the license as revoked. Your financial data is not touched: everything stays accessible, editable and exportable; only the free plan limits apply again to new items.
  • Purpose and legal basis: this data is used only to validate, renew and revoke the purchased license (performance of a contract, art. 7, V, of Brazil’s General Data Protection Law, LGPD). It is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Processor and international transfer: Cloudflare acts as a processor and may process requests outside Brazil. The service does not keep access logs (IP address) beyond Cloudflare’s processing of each request.
  • Retention: the link between purchase and space is kept while Premium exists (including revoked purchases, to prevent improper reactivation); licenses replaced by a transfer, for 28 days; refund notices, for 90 days; if a refund arrives before the purchase was linked to a space, the purchase hash and date are kept to prevent activation.

2.6 Information that reaches us through Apple, support and this website

  • Apple gives the developer App Store sales reports and statistics and, when you turned on sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, crash and usage reports. This follows Apple’s privacy policy.
  • The rating request is Apple’s own prompt: your rating and review go to the App Store.
  • If you write to support, we receive your email address and your message, used to answer you.
  • This website uses no cookies or analytics tools. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, according to GitHub’s policy.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect usage data or use analytics tools.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not connect to banks or financial services.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties. iCloud is used only for sharing between the two of you, the spreadsheet only leaves the iPhone when you share it, and the license service (section 2.5) receives only the purchase data described above.
  • It does not ask for access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

4. Deletion

  • Transactions, accounts, goals and cards can be deleted one by one. With sharing on, deletion applies to both iPhones. Deleting an account also deletes its transactions; deleting a card removes its purchases and adjustments (statement payments stay in the accounts).
  • Stopping sharing or removing the other person (section 2.3) does not delete data from any iPhone.
  • Data on the iPhone: deleting the app deletes the data stored on that iPhone (system iPhone backups may contain a copy).
  • Data in iCloud: in this version the app has no option to delete the whole shared space, or the data stored in iCloud, at once. For help with removal, write to paulotell27@yahoo.com.br. Because the developer has no access to your iCloud, removal is done on the iPhone itself or through Apple’s iCloud settings.

License data: to ask for access to or deletion of the data kept by the license service, write to paulotell27@yahoo.com.br with the Ambos code shown in Settings. Because the service stores no name, email or Apple ID, this code is the only link to the purchase. After deletion, the current license stops renewing within 14 days.

5. Children

Ambos Money is not directed to children.

6. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. If the app starts handling new data, this page will be updated before that version reaches the App Store.
