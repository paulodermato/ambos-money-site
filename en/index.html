Your data stays yours

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your financial data lives on your iPhones and, when you share, in iCloud, visible only to the person you invite. It never goes through Ambos Money servers. Only the Premium purchase confirmation goes to a small license service, with no amounts, names or email.

Good to know

  • iPhone only (iOS 17 or later). Sharing needs iCloud turned on for both of you.
  • Manual entry. No bank import, by design.
  • It doesn’t split bills or calculate who owes whom. It shows who paid what.
  • One currency per Ambos: US dollar, euro, British pound, Brazilian real or Mexican peso. Amounts are never converted.

Questions

Does Ambos Money connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourselves in a few taps. That means there is no bank connection to break and no credentials to share.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to start (30 transactions a month, 2 accounts and 1 goal, with sharing and CSV export included). Ambos Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks both iPhones.

Does it work on Android?

Not today. Ambos Money is iPhone only, and sharing needs iCloud on both iPhones.

Does it split bills or tell us who owes whom?

No. It shows who paid what, but it does not calculate settlements.

What happens if we don’t buy Premium?

You keep using the free plan. Everything you recorded stays, and you can still view, edit, sync and export. New items follow the free limits.

Can we take our data with us?

Yes. Export accounts, income and expenses as CSV to open in Numbers or Excel, any time and for free.
